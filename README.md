# SAPC
Proteins are composed of amino acids, which serve as their fundamental building blocks. Protein classification based on function and structure is determined by the specific arrangement of these amino acids. Understanding the amino acid sequences and compositions is essential for deciphering protein functions. This study conducts a statistical analysis of all protein chains available in the Protein Data Bank (PDB), offering insights into their amino acid distributions and associated functions. A key focus is investigating that how the absence of specific amino acids in a protein or chain correlates with its function. Researchers can use this data to identify the protein chains containing anywhere from zero to the maximum number of a given amino acid, along with their functional implications. By revealing the patterns between amino acid composition and protein behavior, this work provides a novel perspective for analyzing protein chains. This study will be an aid to understand more about protein chains in drug development, protein evolution studies, and broader functional exploration, opening new avenues for biological research.
